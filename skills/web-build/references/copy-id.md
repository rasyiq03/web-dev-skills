# Writing Indonesian copy

Write the way the owner would describe the business to a friend who might become a
customer: concrete, warm, and short.

- **Lead with a fact from the brief.** "Kopi susu gula aren dari biji Pangalengan, disangrai
  tiap Senin." beats any sentence that could describe every cafe.
- **Concrete verbs and nouns.** Name the product, the place, the time, the person. Use the
  client's own words from the brief where they exist.
- **Register follows the audience.** Students and young customers: "kamu", short sentences,
  everyday words. Professionals, government, health, finance: "Anda", full sentences, no slang.
  Keep one register on the whole site.
- **Hero in three parts, two sentences at most**: what the business is, for whom (or where),
  and the one action, usually "Chat via WhatsApp". Example: "Kopi susu gula aren untuk
  mahasiswa di Dago. Pesan dulu, ambil tanpa antre."
- **One idea per sentence; one or two sentences per paragraph** in hero and card text.
- **Easy to understand.** Write for someone reading on a phone between other things:
  everyday words over formal ones ("pakai" or "gunakan", not "pergunakanlah"), no jargon,
  no English where a common Indonesian word exists ("pesan", not "order now"). If a sentence
  needs reading twice, split it.
- **Headings say what the section holds**: "Menu", "Jam buka", "Cara pesan",
  "Lokasi". A clever heading is fine only when the section's content stays obvious.
- **Buttons name the action and its result**: "Pesan lewat WhatsApp", "Lihat menu lengkap",
  "Unduh katalog (PDF)".
- **Numbers, prices, awards, and quotes come only from the brief.** Everything else is a
  placeholder: `[[ISI: harga kopi susu]]`, `[[ISI: testimoni pelanggan]]`.
- **Local detail is welcome when stated**: a street, a landmark, a dish name in Sundanese or
  Javanese, the city the business serves.
- Standard Indonesian spelling (EYD V); foreign terms in italics only when no common
  Indonesian word exists.
- **Dashboard labels** are nouns with their unit and period: "Pendapatan (Rp, 30 hari)",
  "Pesanan baru minggu ini". Column headings stay short; a status uses one word
  ("Lunas", "Tertunda", "Batal").
