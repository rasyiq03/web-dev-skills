# Writing English copy

Write the way the owner would describe the business to someone who might become a
customer: concrete, plain, and short.

- **Lead with a fact from the brief.** "Palm-sugar iced coffee from Pangalengan beans,
  roasted every Monday." beats any sentence that could describe every cafe.
- **Concrete verbs and nouns.** Name the product, the place, the time, the person. Reuse the
  client's own words from the brief.
- **One register for the whole site**: conversational for consumer brands, precise and
  formal for professional services.
- **One idea per sentence; one or two sentences per paragraph** in hero and card text.
- **Headings say what the section holds**: "Menu", "Opening hours", "How to order", "Find us".
- **Buttons name the action and its result**: "Order on WhatsApp", "See the full menu",
  "Download the catalog (PDF)".
- **Numbers, prices, awards, and quotes come only from the brief.** Everything else is a
  placeholder: `[[ISI: price of iced coffee]]`, `[[ISI: customer testimonial]]`.
- **Bilingual sites**: write this version from the brief, not as a translation of the
  Indonesian page. Both versions carry the same facts.
- **Dashboard labels** are nouns with their unit and period: "Revenue (IDR, last 30 days)",
  "New orders this week". Column headings stay short; a status uses one word
  ("Paid", "Pending", "Cancelled").
